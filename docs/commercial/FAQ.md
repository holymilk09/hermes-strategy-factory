# FAQ

## What am I subscribing to?
You get a weekly research-only setup sheet called Strategy Factory Edge Sheet.

## How often does it update?
- Monday: full setup report
- Friday: scoreboard update

## Is this investment advice?
No. This is research-only tracking and education.

## Does this send buy/sell alerts?
No. It does not provide trade alerts.

## Is this connected to a broker?
No. There is no broker execution.

## Is this a live trading bot?
No. It is a research-only reporting product.

## What do I receive in each report?
Plain-English setup views, price areas that matter, setup-break levels, and what changed this week.

## Is this personalized to my account?
No. It is not personalized financial advice.
