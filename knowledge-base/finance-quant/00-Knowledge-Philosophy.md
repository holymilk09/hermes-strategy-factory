# Knowledge Philosophy — Thinking with the Vault

**Core Principle**: Storage is cheap. Synthesis is expensive. This vault optimizes for synthesis.

## The Four Layers of Knowledge Use

### Layer 1: Atomic Concepts
Single ideas, cleanly defined. Examples:
- "Rough volatility" 
- "Kyle's lambda"
- "False discovery rate in multiple testing"

### Layer 2: Cross-Domain Connections
Where real edges live. Examples:
- Connecting information theory (entropy) to market regime detection
- Linking microstructure theory to practical order execution algorithms
- Relating evolutionary biology concepts to strategy adaptation

### Layer 3: Mental Models & First Principles
Reusable reasoning frameworks. These are more valuable than raw facts.

### Layer 4: Anti-Fragile Insights
Notes that improve with criticism and real-world feedback. These go into [[00-Anti-Cookie-Cutter-Insights]].

## How to Reason with This System

When facing a trading problem, deliberately traverse multiple layers:

1. What mathematical structures are underneath this problem?
2. What statistical traps or inference issues exist?
3. What market microstructure realities affect it?
4. What behavioral elements are at play?
5. Has this pattern appeared in other contexts (physics, biology, etc.)?

**Rule of Thumb**: If a connection isn't obvious at first, it's probably valuable.

## Anti-Fragility Rules

- Every new piece of knowledge must answer: "How does this make me less wrong?"
- Actively seek disconfirming evidence for popular ideas.
- Maintain "failure mode" sections in strategy notes.
- Regularly review old notes with new eyes.

---

*This system rewards depth of connection over breadth of collection.*
