# Risk Metrics

## Portfolio risk

- Gross exposure
- Net exposure
- Leverage
- Margin utilization
- Concentration / Herfindahl index
- Sector exposure
- Factor exposure
- Beta exposure
- Correlation concentration
- Liquidity-adjusted exposure

## Loss risk

- Max drawdown
- Expected shortfall
- Stress loss
- Gap risk
- Risk of ruin
- Worst-day/worst-week/worst-month loss

## Dynamic risk controls

- Volatility targeting
- Drawdown-based de-risking
- Position cap by liquidity
- Symbol cap
- Sector cap
- Max order size
- Max daily loss
- Kill switch
