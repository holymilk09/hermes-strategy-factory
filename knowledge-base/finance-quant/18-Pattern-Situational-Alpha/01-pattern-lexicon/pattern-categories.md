# Pattern Categories for Research

## A. Trend-State Patterns
- 20 MA > 50 MA, both rising, spread expanding
- Higher highs/lows sequence
- Shallow pullbacks holding above 20 MA
Purpose: regime filter, not entry signal
Key academic: Brock, Lakonishok, LeBaron (1992) — MA rules on DJ data 1897-1986

## B. Expansion/Contraction Patterns
- Large momentum candle → range contraction → volume dry-up → tight closes → breakout
Includes: Qullamaggie-style first pullback after momentum expansion
Key paper: Lo, Mamaysky, Wang — kernel regression pattern recognition 1962-1996

## C. Reversal Patterns
- Climax volume, downside rejection wick, failed breakdown, undercut/reclaim
Purpose: mean reversion candidates

## D. Calendar/Situational Patterns
- Friday/Thursday/Monday price-path claims
- Turn-of-month, FOMC drift, CPI day, earnings drift
- OpEx week, quarter-end, holiday effects
Key research: Lucca & Moench (pre-FOMC drift), McConnell & Xu (turn-of-month)

## E. Time-of-Day Patterns
- Opening range breakout, first 30-min reversal
- Lunch liquidity fade, power hour trend continuation
- Close auction imbalance, overnight vs intraday split
Key research: Heston, Korajczyk, Sadka (intraday return continuation)

## F. Theme/Sector Leadership
- Relative strength ranking within theme baskets
- Identify leaders, then narrow to clean setups
Failure mode: crowding, late-stage exhaustion, RS = exhaustion signal

## G. Cross-Asset / Correlation
- Stocks vs bonds, stocks vs VIX, growth vs rates
- Gold vs real yields, semis vs Nasdaq, DXY vs commodities
Purpose: risk filter, confirmation layer, diversification detection

## Candlestick Reality
Stand-alone named candlesticks are WEAK. Research (Marshall et al.) shows candlestick TA alone is not profitable on US equities.
Value comes when combined with: trend context + support/resistance + volume state + higher-timeframe alignment.
